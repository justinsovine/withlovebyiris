# Memo 12: Three buyers on a phone

Site as revised: 12 SKUs, four categories, guest checkout with pickup and flat rate, bear inquiry with deposit, no `/booth`, no bundles.

## Buyer A: Amelia, 58, church bazaar QR, reorder Lavender plus a candle for her sister, pickup

Scans the QR at the booth, lands on the homepage. First thought: "This says With Love by Iris. My bar says Wholesome Living. Same lady?" She scrolls, sees the workbench-free hero photo and "made in Bethel, Ohio", decides yes. Taps Shop, then Bath & Body, then the shampoo bar. Scent pills: Lavender Fields is there. Adds it. Backs out to Candles & Prayer, reads the three verses, picks Psalm 23 for her sister. Cart, checkout. Chooses "Local pickup in Bethel (free)". **Hesitation:** the phone field appears and nothing else. "Pick up where? When? Do I knock on her door?" She almost texts Iris instead of paying. If the pickup option reads "Pickup in Bethel, free. I text you when it's ready, usually 2 days. Porch box or meet at the Bethel post office," she pays. She also never saw a gift note field because it is on the shipping step and she picked pickup. **Completes**, but only if pickup copy exists.

## Buyer B: Columbus, 41, hospice card, memory bear from her father's flannels for her mother by Christmas

Types the URL from the card. Homepage sells shampoo bars; she scrolls past. Taps Shop, sees Custom Sewing, opens Memory Bears. Three bears with stories; she cries a little, which is the page working. Reads "$195, one bear a month, 4 to 6 weeks after your clothing arrives." **Hesitation:** it is September 10. "Is Christmas possible? Is this month booked already? Do I mail the shirts before or after she says yes?" The form asks for a target date but gives no answer back. She opens Etsy in another tab to compare. What holds her: a live line above the form, "Next open slot: October. For Christmas, clothing must reach me by November 1. I reply to every inquiry within 2 days," plus a note under the mail option, "Send 2 to 3 shirts, USPS Ground, I confirm when they arrive." Deposit by emailed draft order is fine for her; the wait for a reply is not, unless the form promises one. **Completes the inquiry** with the slot copy; drops off without it.

## Buyer C: Cincinnati, 34, Facebook share, beard balm for father-in-law, Apple Pay, never heard of the brand

Lands directly on "Strong & Steadfast". Sees the jar, the verse, sandalwood. Good. **Hesitation one:** "How big is the jar? Is $X for 2 oz or 4 oz?" No size on the page. **Hesitation two:** "Who is this?" He scrolls to the footer, sees Bethel, Ohio and an email, opens Our Story, reads the faith paragraph, comes back. Compares to "The Noble Man"; the one-line scent notes settle it. Adds to cart, taps the Apple Pay button above the card form. **Hesitation three:** the sheet shows "Local pickup in Bethel" as the first shipping method; he is 30 miles away and pickup makes no sense from a wallet sheet. If USPS flat rate is the default for any address outside Bethel and pickup sits as a toggle above the wallet button, he pays in eight seconds. **Completes** if size and default shipping are fixed.
