# Marketing memo: With Love by Iris

Panelist: marketing strategist, faith-based and community-rooted small brands. Experience in the memory bear and keepsake grief market, Facebook-first audiences of women 45 to 70.

## 1. Positioning and name

One sentence: **"Handmade in Bethel, Ohio, with scripture and love: comfort for your body, your home, and your memories."** Body (Wholesome Living bath and body), home (beeswax prayer candles), memories (memory bears and baby quilts). The buyer is the same woman in all three cases: a 45 to 70 year old who buys gifts for her family and church friends. Comfort is the thread, not the product category.

Name: **With Love by Iris** as the house brand, **Wholesome Living** as the bath and body line only. Reasons: "Wholesome Living" cannot hold a grief keepsake without sounding like a wellness blog. "With Love by Iris" works on a memory bear tag, a candle lid, and a shampoo bar. Her labels already say "by ir!s", so nothing is wasted. Keep the "ir!s" stylization on labels; use plain "Iris" in URLs, email, and Facebook page name so people can search for it.

## 2. The memory bear is the flagship

Market reality: established makers charge $100 to $175 for 10 to 18 inch bears (TammyBears price list), $150 to $200 with fur accents, and The Patchwork Bear runs order windows with price increases when the window closes. Iris should price at **$135 for a 16 inch bear, $165 with photo patch, $40 per additional bear from the same garment lot**. Estimating: her seamstress skill justifies the top of that range, and a grieving family does not shop on price.

Referral partners, in order of yield:
1. **Funeral homes** in Clermont County (Amelia, Batavia, Bethel, New Richmond area). Ask to leave a small framed card and 20 brochures in the arrangement room. Offer the director a free bear for their own family so they can speak from experience. Funeral homes already sell keepsake jewelry; a local bear is an easy add.
2. **Hospice** social workers and chaplains (Hospice of Cincinnati serves Clermont). They meet families before the death. One relationship here beats any ad.
3. **Churches**: her own congregation first, then bereavement ministries. A short testimony post in the church Facebook group after each bear.
4. **Estate sale companies** and thrift consignment: they see the closets. Give them a referral card with a $15 thank-you gift.

Site presentation, on one page at /memory-bears:
- Open with one real story and one real bear photo, not a grid.
- Process in five steps: send or drop off 1 to 3 garments, Iris calls you, she sews, you approve a photo, pickup or ship.
- Pricing table, plainly stated. Lead time stated (4 to 6 weeks, longer in November and December).
- **50 percent deposit** at order, balance on approval photo. Son: build this as an inquiry form plus a deposit product, not a normal cart item.
- A line about what happens to leftover fabric (returned or made into a keepsake pillow for $35, an upsell that also honors the garment).

## 3. Facebook is the primary channel

Organic reach for pages is 1.6 to 5.9 percent of followers per post in 2026 numbers, but small focused pages beat that, and her audience lives on Facebook. Her long process posts are already the right format. Cadence she can sustain, four posts a week, all from the workbench phone:

- Monday: **process post** (long, her voice, ingredient or fabric story). This is the one that converts; keep writing them.
- Wednesday: **one product, one price, one link**. Short.
- Friday: **customer photo or testimonial**, with permission. Memory bear reveals go here.
- Sunday: **verse of the week** tied to a candle or balm. Faith posts get the most shares in this demographic.

Post from her personal profile too, sharing the page post. Personal profiles reach far more than pages. Go live from the workbench once a month; no editing.

Integration: connect the site catalog to **Facebook Shop** through Meta Commerce Manager so products tag in posts and check out on the site. Skip Marketplace except for character bears and ready-made quilts, which sell well there locally with pickup.

## 4. Email

Use **MailerLite** free plan: 250 subscribers and 2,500 sends per month after the June 2026 change. Mailchimp's free tier is also 250 contacts but only 500 sends and counts unsubscribes. She will not hit 250 for a while; when she does, the paid tier is about $10 a month.

Welcome sequence, three emails: (1) who Iris is and a verse, sent immediately, with a 10 percent first-order code; (2) day 3, the story of one memory bear; (3) day 7, the shampoo bar scent guide. Then at most **two campaigns a month**: one product or seasonal, one story. Signup at checkout, at the booth via a QR code, and a footer form.

## 5. Local marketing

- Church bulletins in Bethel, Amelia, and Batavia: a $25 to $50 bulletin ad is worth more than any online ad for her.
- Facebook community groups for Bethel, Amelia, Batavia, and the Clermont County buy/sell groups. Post as a neighbor, one time a week, never more.
- Clermont County Fair (July), Bethel and Amelia fall festivals, church craft bazaars in November. Collect emails at every booth.
- The vet: give them the Paw-Fection balm on consignment at a set price; do not eat a loss beyond the first case.
- Two boutiques in Milford or Batavia on consignment for candles and balms, one gift shop near a funeral home.

## 6. What not to do

- No paid ads until 20 organic site orders exist. Ads amplify what already sells; she does not know that yet.
- No TikTok. Her buyers are not there and short video would cost her the hours she needs to sew.
- No influencer chasing. Her influencers are pastors' wives and funeral directors.
- Do not spread the bath and body line wider. Ten shampoo scents is already too many for a solo maker; the panel should cut to six.

## 7. 90-day launch plan

**Days 1 to 30, foundation.** Name reconciled. Site live with candles, balms, six shampoo scents, and the memory bear inquiry page. MailerLite welcome sequence built. Facebook page renamed, shop connected. Ten memory bear brochures printed.

**Days 31 to 60, referrals.** Visit three funeral homes, one hospice, and her own church. Post four times a week. Booth at one fall event with a QR code and email signup. First two email campaigns go out.

**Days 61 to 90, proof.** Publish three memory bear stories with permission. Consignment placed at the vet and one boutique. Holiday order window announced for memory bears with a firm cutoff of November 15. Review what sold and cut what did not.
