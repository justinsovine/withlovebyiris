## 2. Best demand-to-competition ratio in her range

1. Paw-Fection paw balm. Category growing 6.2%, retailer-confirmed clean-label demand, and she already has a wholesale path (the vet). Tallow plus beeswax is on-trend. Price it at $12 to 14; selling at a loss is unjustified when the market band starts at $10.
2. Scripture beard balms. 6.8% growth, balm preferred over oil, and the faith-forward men's gift angle has almost no direct competitors. Her most defensible product for a direct-to-site store.
3. Unscented 100% beeswax candles. Fastest-growing wax, and "light it while you pray" turns "no fragrance" into the point. Soy is where the saturation is; she is not in that lane.

Shampoo bars have the biggest market but the worst ratio: Etsy price compression to $6 to 9, and ten scents fragment her volume.

## 3. Two adjacent products to add

1. Whipped tallow balm, unscented plus one light scent. Fastest-rising search term in her category, she already buys tallow, and unscented is what retailers report demand for. $22 to 28 per 4 oz (estimate). https://www.fortunebusinessinsights.com/tallow-balm-market-112499
2. Tallow or shea solid lotion bar. Same ingredients, wrap, and label as the shampoo bars; cross-sells as a "bar set." Demand inferred from Etsy market pages (estimate). https://www.etsy.com/market/tallow_solid_balm_bar

## 4. Seasonality

- Tallow and balms: January peak on winter-dryness searches. Stock November to February. https://teestallow.com/beef-tallow-skincare-trend-2026-what-the-latest-research-really-says/
- Candles and beard balms: Q4 gifting and Father's Day (June). Build in September and April (estimate).
- Paw balm: winter salt and July pavement, two pushes per year (estimate).
- Shampoo bars: flat, mild Q4 lift (estimate).

## 5. Stop or shrink

- Lip gloss roll-ons: flat, commodity priced, high effort per dollar. Gift sets only.
- Clay mask and facial scrub: flat, mass-brand competition, preservation risk. Booth-only or drop.
- Cut shampoo bars to 4 to 5 scents (estimate).
