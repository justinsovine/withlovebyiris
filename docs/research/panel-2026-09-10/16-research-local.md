# Local demand: greater Cincinnati / Clermont County, 2025 to 2026

Research window: about 6 minutes, 8 searches. Suburban fair vendor lists (Milford, Loveland, Anderson, Batavia) were not reachable in that window; claims about them are marked as estimates.

## 1. What sells locally, with evidence

- Cincinnati's biggest juried indie show, Crafty Supermarket, runs two shows a year at Music Hall with 100+ makers; the organizer's own category list is "ceramics, jewelry, original art, home decor, apparel, paper goods, self-care products, and thoughtful gifts" (https://craftysupermarket.com/upcoming-shows/, https://craftysupermarket.com/). Self-care is a named category, not a filler one.
- Findlay Market has a permanent beeswax merchant, Bee Haven, whose product line (100% beeswax candles, beeswax lanterns, hand salves, lip balms) overlaps Iris's candle and balm line almost exactly, and whose "country bees" are in Clermont County (https://www.findlaymarket.org/merchant/beehaven, https://beehavenhoney.com/our-products/). A year-round stall in Cincinnati's flagship market is the strongest local demand signal for beeswax goods.
- Cincinnati Magazine's "natural skin care" roundup is all soap, scrub, body butter, and balm makers (Lemonwood, Minor Obsession, &Sundries, Queen City Alchemy, Earthganics, Oakley Soap Co.); notably none of the six leads with shampoo bars (https://www.cincinnatimagazine.com/article/six-local-businesses-that-make-natural-skin-care-and-hygiene-products/, article dated 2020).
- Buyer profile: Clermont County median age 40.8, median household income $85,510 (2024), projected population 214,037 in 2026 (https://www.ohio-demographics.com/clermont-county-demographics, https://www.point2homes.com/US/Neighborhood/OH/Clermont-County-Demographics.html, https://worldpopulationreview.com/us-counties/ohio/clermont-county). No county-level church attendance or pet ownership figure was found; treat the faith-forward fit as a qualitative bet, not a measured one.

### Soap at Cincinnati fairs

The son's "everyone does soap" is true at church and school bazaars (estimate, not verified) but false at the region's juried show. Crafty Supermarket vendor lists:

- Spring 2026: 127 vendors. Soap/bath/skincare 3 (Exquisitely Whipped, Nuni Products, Simply Sol Care). Candles 1 (Melanin Flame, soy). Sewn/quilt/plush 6 (Quilt Rescue, Stufficorn, the Savage Stitcher, Tuft Itties, Crochet by Cate, Little Bike Bag). Pet 1 (Kora's Kitchen Pets). (https://craftysupermarket.com/2026-spring-vendors/)
- Holiday 2025: 136 vendors. Soap/bath 2 (Bomani Beauty MD, Simply Sol Care). Candles 1 (Melanin Flame, soy). Sewn/plush 6. Pet 1 (Pup and the Pepper). (https://craftysupermarket.com/2025-holiday-makers/)

Zero beeswax candle vendors and zero memory-keepsake vendors at either show. Implication: at a curated show, lead the table with scripture beeswax candles and a memory bear sample with an inquiry card; shampoo bars are the traffic item, not the headline. At uncurated suburban bazaars, assume soap saturation (estimate) and lead the same way.

## 2. Named local competitors per category

- Shampoo bars: PT Soap Co. (Cincinnati, shampoo bars plus solid dish soap and steamers, https://www.ptsoapco.com/), Lemonwood Soap Co., Tree Hugger Soap Co. (https://www.thetreehuggerco.com/), Buff City Soap chain. Crowded in soap, moderately crowded in shampoo bars. No Clermont County shampoo bar maker surfaced (search found none).
- Beeswax candles: Bee Haven (Findlay Market and Northside), Hive and Comb (local beekeeper, https://www.hiveandcomb.com/shop). Two serious players; both are beekeepers first, candles second, neither faith-branded. Soy candle makers (Nova, Manitou) are many but a different product.
- Memory bears: no Cincinnati-area maker surfaced. Ohio's Hospice affiliates serving Clermont, Brown, and Adams counties give volunteer-made bears free to patient families (https://www.ohioshospice.org/cch-and-ohfc-offer-memory-bears/). Paid competition is out of state (Patchwork Bear, NJ). Uncrowded.
- Dog paw balm: Modern Herb: Pet (started at Applehead City Pet, Bellevue KY, https://www.appleheadcitypet.com/). One local branded competitor.

## 3. Gaps: local demand, few local makers

1. Beeswax candles with a faith angle. Only two local beeswax makers and none at either 2025 to 2026 Crafty Supermarket show; the beekeepers sell honey-first, unscented, no scripture (https://craftysupermarket.com/2026-spring-vendors/, https://beehavenhoney.com/our-products/).
2. Paid memory bears and birth-detail baby quilts. No paid Cincinnati maker found; the only regional supply is free hospice volunteers, which proves demand exists and that grieving families here already know the product (https://www.ohioshospice.org/cch-and-ohfc-offer-memory-bears/).
3. Men's scripture beard balm. None of the six Cincinnati Magazine makers or the Crafty vendors sells a men's line (https://www.cincinnatimagazine.com/article/six-local-businesses-that-make-natural-skin-care-and-hygiene-products/).

Shampoo bars and soap are not a gap.

## 4. Three local retail or partner targets

1. MiCA 12/v, Over-the-Rhine: stocks fine craft, local art, "apothecary items", baby and children's gifts (https://www.shopmica.com/). Pitch candles and baby quilt commissions.
2. Applehead City Pet, Bellevue KY: grooming spa with a boutique that explicitly stocks locally made, ethically created pet care (https://www.appleheadcitypet.com/). Pitch Paw-Fection; also Pooch & Purr, which advertises Ohio-made shampoo products (https://www.pooch-and-purr.com/).
3. Community Care Hospice (serves Clermont County): partner, not retailer. Offer paid upgrades (photo patch, quilt) to families beyond the free volunteer bear, or become a referral maker (https://www.ohioshospice.org/cch-and-ohfc-offer-memory-bears/).

## 5. What the local data contradicts

A "handmade bath products online" plan puts shampoo bars at the center. Locally, shampoo bars are her most crowded category (PT Soap, Lemonwood, Tree Hugger, Buff City, and 2 to 3 bath vendors per juried show) while her least crowded categories, beeswax scripture candles and memory bears, had zero peers on 263 combined vendor slots across the last two Crafty Supermarket shows (https://craftysupermarket.com/2026-spring-vendors/, https://craftysupermarket.com/2025-holiday-makers/). The site should be built around the uncontested items (candles, keepsake inquiry flow) with bath goods as the repeat-purchase add-on, not the reverse.
