# Etsy memo: use it as a funnel for two product lines, nothing else

Panelist: marketplace strategist. Numbers below are 2026 US Etsy fees confirmed by search; margin math uses my estimates of Iris's costs and is labeled as such.

## 1. Fee stack, two real orders

Etsy 2026 fees: $0.20 listing (per listing, renews on each sale), 6.5% transaction on item plus shipping, Etsy Payments 3% + $0.25, one-time shop setup fee ($15 to $29, confirm at signup). Offsite Ads: 15% of an attributed sale if she opts in, mandatory at 12% once the shop passes $10k/year. She will be under $10k, so opt out.

**$12 shampoo bar, $5 shipping charged ($17 gross)**
- Etsy fees: $0.20 + $1.11 + $0.76 = **$2.07** (12% of gross, 17% of the item price)
- Real USPS Ground Advantage from Bethel, 4 oz: ~$5.25, plus mailer ~$0.50 (estimate)
- Materials and label: ~$3 (estimate)
- Net: **~$6.20** before her time. With Offsite Ads on: ~$3.65.
- Verdict: a single bar is not an Etsy product. The shippable unit is a 3-bar set at $32.

**$150 memory bear, $15 shipping charged ($165 gross)**
- Etsy fees: $0.20 + $10.73 + $5.20 = **$16.13** (9.8% of gross)
- Real shipping, 2 lb box: ~$14 plus box $2 (estimate); materials (stuffing, joints, photo patch print, thread): ~$18 (estimate)
- Net: **~$115** for 4 to 6 hours of work, roughly $20 to $28/hr
- Same sale on her own site with Stripe (2.9% + $0.30): fees are $5.09. Etsy costs her **$11 more per bear**. That is a cheap customer acquisition cost for a $150 order from a buyer who searched with intent.

Pricing check: Etsy memory bears cluster at $79 (plain), $119 (embroidered), $145+ (handwriting, photo patch). Her $150 is top of band. Tier it: $95 / $135 / $165, and make the photo patch the visible reason for the top tier.

Bonus for a non-technical seller: Etsy is a marketplace facilitator and collects and remits Ohio sales tax for her. On her own site the son has to wire Stripe Tax.

## 2. Zero, funnel, or mirror

**Funnel, 3 to 5 listings, hard cap.** Not zero, because withlovebyiris.com has zero search traffic on launch day and Etsy has intent traffic for "memory bear" on day one. Not a mirror, because 20 low-ticket listings bury her under shampoo bar noise, drag her shop-wide stats (shipping speed, reviews) onto the listings that matter, and double her order handling.

Listings: memory bear (2 tiers as separate listings), baby quilt with birth details, scripture beeswax candle set of 3, Strong & Steadfast beard balm framed as a Christian gift for men. That is five. Nothing else.

## 3. Demand reality

- **Memory bears: real.** Grief searches are constant, need-driven, price-insensitive, and reviews decide the sale. Etsy's 2025 to 2026 handmade enforcement (original design required, laser and Cricut template work disqualified as of August 2026, handmade removals quadrupled) is clearing out her competition, not her. A custom sewn bear from the customer's own clothing is the most defensible "handmade" listing on the platform.
- **Baby quilts with applique birth details: real, slower.** Searches spike around births; personalization is the moat. Lead time 4 to 6 weeks is normal.
- **Scripture candles: modest.** "Prayer candle" and "scripture candle" have a following but the top results are printed-label soy candles at $18. Her angle is 100% beeswax, no fragrance, sold as a set. Try it, cut it after 4 months if it has under 5 sales.
- **Beard balm: noise.** Tens of thousands of listings. Only listable because of the verse-on-lid gift angle. Low expectations.
- **Paw balm: do not list.** $10 price, she already takes a loss, and fees plus shipping make every order negative. This is a vet counter and Nextdoor product.
- **Shampoo bars, lip gloss, masks, scrubs: do not list.** Commodity categories, $6 to $9 price competition, single-item shipping kills them.

## 4. Routing Etsy buyers to her site, legally

Etsy's fee avoidance rule prohibits using Etsy (listings, shop announcement, messages, About text) to steer shoppers to another venue to buy the same items. Do not write "cheaper on my website," do not put the URL in listing descriptions, do not send it in a Conversation.

What is allowed and works: the shop About page "Around the web" field may link her website and Facebook. Everything inside the box is hers: her own label, a kraft thank-you card that reads "Wholesome Living by ir!s, withlovebyiris.com, follow the process on Facebook," and a code for 10% off the next order on the site. Also a photo card showing the whole line, since the bear buyer never saw the candles. Do not print "skip Etsy next time." The card sells the brand, the site does the rest.

## 5. Alternatives, ranked for her

1. **Nextdoor (free):** best fit. Memory bears, baby quilts, and paw balm are local, word-of-mouth products. Post in Bethel, Amelia, Batavia, Milford. Local pickup means no shipping math.
2. **Facebook page plus Marketplace (free):** her long process posts are already Facebook's format and her buyers are there. Marketplace listings with local pickup for quilts and bears. Skip Facebook Shop checkout; link every post to the site.
3. **Instagram:** only if she will shoot 30-second Reels at the workbench. Static phone photos will not move it. Low priority.
4. **Faire:** not now. 25% on a retailer's first order, 15% after, and retailers expect wholesale at half of retail. A $12 bar nets her about $4.50. Revisit only if she wants to batch 50 units of a shelf-stable SKU.

## 6. To the son

Half right. Etsy is cutthroat for what you assumed she'd list there: bars, balms, gloss. It is not cutthroat for a seamstress doing bereavement work, where the buyer searches by need, the listing count is in the low thousands, and the 2026 handmade crackdown is pruning the field. The cost of Etsy on a bear is $11 over Stripe. The cost of no Etsy is a new domain with no traffic waiting for your marketing to work. Take the five listings, build the site as the home, and treat every Etsy box as a conversion event to withlovebyiris.com.
