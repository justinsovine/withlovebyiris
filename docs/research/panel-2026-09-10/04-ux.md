# Memo 04: Storefront UX and conversion

Panelist: DTC ecommerce UX and conversion, small handmade brands, Medusa v2 / Next.js.

2026 benchmarks: median Shopify store converts ~1.4%, beauty and personal care 2.5 to 3.5%, mobile 1.2 to 1.9% for small stores while carrying 65 to 75% of traffic. Beauty repeat-purchase rate runs 25 to 40%; half of second orders land inside 30 days, three quarters inside 90. Apple Pay and Google Pay are the single biggest mobile lift. Sources: [Blend Commerce](https://blendcommerce.com/blogs/shopify/ecommerce-conversion-rate-benchmarks-2026), [TryNow](https://trynow.com/blog/beauty-ecommerce-benchmarks-2026), [Taylor Sicard](https://taylorsicard.com/blog/dtc-retention-benchmarks-2026), [Pennock](https://www.pennock.co/blog/how-to-grow-a-haircare-brand-the-2026-dtc-playbook-for-routine-retention-amp-replenishment). Assume a phone screen throughout.

## 1. Information architecture

Seven categories for under 50 SKUs is too many, and the proposed tree mixes audience (For Him, For Pets), type (Candles), and craft (Handmade & Sewn). Collapse to four:

- **Bath & Body** (the Wholesome Living line): shampoo bars, balms, mask, scrub, lip gloss, muscle balm. "For Him" is a tag and landing page, not a category.
- **Candles & Prayer**: scripture beeswax candles. Devotional intent, not skincare.
- **Custom Sewing**: memory bears, quilts, character bears. Inquiry-driven.
- **Gifts**: sampler packs, gift sets, Paw-Fection (pets sell as gifts).

Mobile nav: logo, Shop, Our Story, cart. Nothing else.

Homepage above the fold for the QR visitor: one workbench photo of Iris with product, a headline in her voice ("Small batch, made in Bethel, Ohio"), one button "Shop Shampoo Bars", and a strip: "Free pickup in Bethel. Flat $X shipping." Below the fold: Scent Sampler, scripture candle, memory bear teaser. Point the QR at a `/booth` route that auto-applies a 72-hour 10% promo so she can measure booth-to-site conversion.

## 2. Product detail pages

**Shampoo bar**: one product, ten variants under a single "Scent" option. Page order: gallery (bar, wrapped bar, lather on hand), name and price, scent pills with a one-line note under each ("Orange Cream: warm citrus, like a creamsicle"), Add to cart plus a "Try 3 scents" link, then accordions: How to use (wet, 3 to 4 passes, rinse, rest on a draining dish), full ingredients (kaolin clay, argan oil, essential oils, vitamin E, petal dust; no sulfates), "Lasts about 60 to 80 washes" (her verified number), ships in 2 business days. Skip the scent quiz; good scent notes do 80% of the job with zero build.

**Scripture candle**: variant is the verse. Show the lid large, burn time, "100% beeswax, no fragrance, no soot", one paragraph in her words about praying while it burns. Cross-sell the matching men's balm; the verse pairings already exist on the labels.

**Memory bear**: not add-to-cart. Show three finished bears with their stories (with family permission), a price range ("most bears $X to $Y"), lead time ("4 to 6 weeks after your clothing arrives"), and "Start a bear". Form collects: name, email, phone, whose clothing and relationship (free text, she needs it for the note she writes), bear count, photo patch yes/no, names or dates to embroider, target date, drop-off in Bethel or mail. On submit: email Iris, confirm to customer. She quotes by reply; the son creates a Medusa draft order for a 50% deposit and emails the payment link, then a second draft order for the balance at completion. Never take full payment before she has seen the clothing.

## 3. Checkout

Guest by default; account creation is a post-payment checkbox ("Save your details for reorders"). Two shipping methods: "Local pickup in Bethel (free)" and "USPS flat rate $X". Pickup swaps the address form for a phone field and shows editable pickup instructions. Stripe Payment Element with wallets on, wallet button above the card form. One notes field ("Gift message or anything I should know") on the shipping step. Tax line visible (origin-based Ohio rate, Clermont County). Promo field visible, collapsed, one tap. Two steps total.

## 4. Retention

Three plain-text emails in her voice: confirmation; "shipped / ready for pickup" with the how-to-use card; day 14 "How's the lather?" asking for a review. Reorder reminder at day 55 for a single bar, day 100 for a sampler, with a one-tap link prefilled to the last scent ordered.

Bundles as their own Medusa products with inventory kits so component stock decrements: Scent Sampler (3 half bars, choose scents), Gift Set (bar plus candle in the honeycomb bag), Men's Set (balm plus candle).

Skip subscriptions in v1.

Loyalty: one mechanic. "Bring a friend" code: friend gets 10% off, referrer gets a handwritten thank-you and a free lip gloss in their next order.

## 5. Trust signals

Our Story page: two workbench photos, her sewing history, one honest paragraph on faith. Ingredients page listing every ingredient and why, linked from every PDP. Reviews from the day-14 email, rendered on the PDP, no third-party widget. Footer: Bethel, Ohio, email, "Made by hand, ships in 2 days, pickup free."

## 6. Build order

1. Shampoo bar PDP with scent variants, cart, guest checkout with pickup and flat rate, Stripe wallets on.
2. `/booth` QR route with auto-applied promo.
3. Transactional emails plus the day-14 review ask and day-55 reorder reminder.
4. Memory bear inquiry form plus draft-order deposit flow.
5. Sampler and gift set bundles with inventory kits.

Skip in v1: required accounts, scent quiz, subscriptions, points, wishlist, chat, blog. Revisit subscriptions only if reorder-link clicks exceed 20% of reminder sends.
