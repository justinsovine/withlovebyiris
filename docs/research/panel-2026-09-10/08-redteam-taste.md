# Red team: taste and curation

Judged from the photos as a buyer, not as a friend.

## 1. Excellent and sellable as-is

- **Shampoo bar labels.** The watercolor botanical wreaths with the scent illustrated (pinecones, lime, sandalwood sticks) are the best design decision in the line. This is the brand. Everything else should be pulled toward it.
- **The corduroy postal bear.** Correct proportions, a real snout, black bead eyes set close, one fabric, one uniform. Character bears in this register (mail carrier, nurse, farmer) are a product line, not a one-off.
- **Memory bear construction.** Seams, stuffing, and posture are professional. Skill is not the problem.
- **Baby quilt birth-stat clouds.** Handwritten details stitched into applique clouds is a real heirloom idea with a clear buyer.

## 2. What reads as hobby-craft

- **Label consistency.** Shampoo bars say "Wholesome Living by ir!s" in serif and script. Candles use a rounded handwriting font. Lip gloss uses bold sans on a green rectangle. Three products, three brands. Pick the shampoo bar system and apply it to everything.
- **Candle lids.** Matte black tins are good. The sticker is an orange clipart flame with a cross in the center, the kind you would find in a VBS handout. It fights the botanical line and cheapens a pure beeswax product. Replace with a cream label, the verse reference in the serif, a single thin gold line.
- **The "ir!s" wordmark.** The exclamation-point i reads as a 2009 MySpace handle. Set the name in the label serif as "Iris" and let the watercolor carry personality.
- **Lip gloss.** Green tube, green paper label, "Natural alternative" wrapped around the side: it looks like an essential-oil roller from a MLM starter kit. Clear or amber tubes with a small botanical label, or drop the product from the site.
- **Kraft wraps.** Fine in concept, but the crumpled pleats show in every photo. Fold once with a crisp seam on the underside, or use a paper band.
- **Bee bags.** Three different bee prints, one with gnomes. They are party-supply bags and they say "party favor," not "gift." Plain kraft or cream bags with one sticker beat all three.
- **Punny copy.** "Bee leave me" and "RUFF day" are fine on Facebook, where her regulars know her. On a product page a stranger reads them as a Cricut vinyl shop. Keep the warmth, cut the puns from product titles; allow one per description at most.

## 3. The memory bear collage

**A stranger would buy:** the pink floral bear with the tulle skirt and rosette; the small tan bear family in shirts and the red dress; the corduroy bear.

**Only a family member loves:** the two plaid bears with iron-on face photos on the chest and XOXO hearts on the feet; the purple striped bear with handwriting on the feet; the cheerleader bear with mismatched button eyes and drawn eyebrows.

**The rule:** the body should be one calm fabric and the memory should be worn as clothing or a single accent. When the loud memory fabric becomes the whole body, or a photo of the deceased is printed on the belly, it becomes a memorial object, not a bear. Faces need two matched small dark eyes and a stitched nose; anything else looks unfinished. One accessory, never three.

## 4. Three taste rules the son can enforce quietly

1. **One label system gets a product page.** If the packaging is not in the watercolor botanical system, the product is "available at markets" only. No insult, just a site standard.
2. **Photos define the catalog.** Every listing shot is cream or linen background, natural window light, one hero angle plus one detail. Nothing on sequin fabric or the pegboard bench goes live.
3. **Custom work shows examples he chose.** The memory bear page carries four curated bears and an inquiry form. She still makes whatever a family asks for; the site simply shows the best.

## 5. Reshoot list, phone only

- Shampoo bars: single bar, top-down, on a cream linen napkin near a window, plus a lathered-hand shot.
- Candles: one lit tin, dim room, verse readable, after the label redesign.
- Postal bear: seated on a white stool, plain wall, eye-level, window to one side.
- Baby quilt: flat on a bed made with white sheets, shot from directly above, then one close-up of a cloud.
- Use portrait mode off, tap to focus, lock exposure, no flash, edit only brightness.

## 6. Where her instinct is right

The scripture candles. Unscented pure beeswax, "light it while you pray and read," a verse per tin. A design panel will want to soften the faith or scent the wax. That would be wrong. Devotional buyers are loyal, underserved by tasteful products, and gift in multiples. The concept is the strongest in the catalog; only the sticker needs to change.
