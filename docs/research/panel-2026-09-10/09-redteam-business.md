# Red team: is there a business here?

Numbers from the finance memo unless marked (est.).

## 1. Hobby with a website

The panel's own conservative year is $14,026 revenue, $7,070 before owner pay, on about 1,300 hours. That is $5.44 an hour. A business pays its owner $20 an hour and still clears something. That needs roughly $35k: 30 fairs at $500, 40 online orders a month, 24 bears. Every input is 2x to 4x today. Odds she gets there in 12 months with no live site and no bear pipeline: under 15% (est.). The bath and body line is a $5.50 net product that needs 1,300 unit sales to matter. It will not.

## 2. Five ways this dies in 12 months

1. **SKU creep.** Bars became ten scents, face products, a dog balm, and a postal bear in one season. The panel "cut" her to five scents and assigned nobody to enforce it. Expect 14 by spring.
2. **Justin is the business.** Site, photos, emails, promo routes, draft orders, stock kits. A free engineer with a day job. When he gets busy, the site rots.
3. **Fairs eat everything.** $350 gross, $45 fee, 8 hours plus drive and prep is about $30 an hour before COGS, $18 after. The panel's own kill line is $30. Half these fairs are already dead.
4. **Bears do not batch.** Six bears is $900. The upside case is 24, which is 96 hours of custom work with a dead husband's shirts on the table. That is emotional labor, not a lever.
5. **Cash goes into supplies.** Launch stock is roughly $900 of materials (est.) against a $600 reserve rule. One bulk beeswax order breaks it in week one.

## 3. Where the panel went soft

- Six experts and a $7k year. Nobody said a Square site would do 90% of this in a weekend.
- The 25-hour week is asserted, never checked. If she works 12 now, the plan is fiction.
- Free bears for funeral directors: four hours and $16 per "partner," before any referral. Give a brochure.
- Consignment and wholesale for the same vet is two policies for one customer. Consignment is unpaid inventory in someone else's building.
- Etsy "billboard": five listings, no sales history, ads off. Etsy search will not surface her. That is a listing fee, not a billboard.
- "Bears are the real business" and "bars are the business" appear in the same document.

## 4. Rules Justin holds her to

- **SKU cap: 12 sellable SKUs including variants.** New one in, one retires.
- **No new product** until 90 days of sales data exist and every unit in stock carries a legal label.
- **Kill rule:** under 10 units in 90 days, or a fair under $30 an hour, is dropped at quarterly review. No appeals.
- **Pricing floor:** 4x materials retail, 2x wholesale, no exceptions. "I'll take the loss" is a veto trigger.
- **Cash rule:** supplies come from the business account only, and only if $600 remains after.

## 5. Survivable on 10 hours a week

Six fairs a year, only ones that already clear $500. One process post a week. Kill Etsy, consignment, gift sets, samplers, the vet, the face line, the postal bear, and the "Find Me At" page. Keep five bars, two balms, candles, and a bear inquiry form capped at one bear a month at $195. Justin builds steps 1, 3, and 4 only. Hours: 4 making, 2 packing, 2 bears, 1 content, 1 admin.

## 6. Verdict

**Fix it.** Spend the $700 on legal and nothing else until she holds the SKU cap for 90 days. What changes my mind: five paid bear deposits at $195 on a waiting list. Then it is a business, and the bars are the gift-shop sideline they always were.
