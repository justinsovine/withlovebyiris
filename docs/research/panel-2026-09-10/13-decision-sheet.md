# With Love by Iris: decision sheet

## Decided

- Name: With Love by Iris is the brand and site, Wholesome Living stays as the bath and body line name, and the wordmark is plain "Iris" in the label serif with the watercolor botanical system on everything.
- Categories: four (Bath & Body, Candles & Prayer, Custom Sewing, Gifts) plus Our Story, with "For Him" as a tag, not a category.
- Catalog: 12 sellable SKUs at launch, which is five shampoo scents plus one seasonal, two men's balms, three candle verses, and one Paw-Fection two-pack; lip gloss, face line, muscle balm, samplers, gift sets, and Find Me At are cut.
- Prices: retail at least 4x materials and wholesale 2x, enforced by the catalog, with bars $12 (3 for $32), balm $18, candles $22 to $24, Paw-Fection $10 at the booth and $6 wholesale to the vet at a 12-unit prepaid minimum.
- Bears: one memory bear per month at $195 with a 50% deposit by draft order, inquiry only, form closes when the month is booked, and five paid deposits on a waiting list is the year-two go or no-go.
- Channels: the site, six fairs a year that already clear $500 gross and $30 an hour after materials, Facebook (one process post a week minimum), funeral home and hospice brochures, and the vet on prepaid wholesale; Etsy, all consignment, paid ads, and Facebook Shop are out for year one.
- Hours: the plan assumes 10 hours a week (4 making, 2 packing, 2 bears, 1 content, 1 admin), and Justin logs her actual hours for four weeks before anything else is planned.
- Site v1 scope: five pieces only, which are catalog, guest checkout with Bethel pickup and $6 flat rate (free over $45) with Stripe wallets, bear inquiry with the deposit flow, three transactional emails, and the day-55 reorder reminder; bundles, subscriptions, accounts, and the /booth route are deferred.

## Before first sale

- Legal: product liability insurance, Ohio county vendor's license plus state transient vendor's license, EIN with a separate business checking account, Stripe under the EIN, and the Ohio LLC once revenue is real; the roughly $700 legal spend precedes the first shipped order and any new product.
- Labels: every cosmetic gets an identity statement, net weight in oz and grams, full INCI ingredient list, business name and address, and a contact for adverse events on a back label or hang tag, with no drug claims, ASTM F2058 caution text on candle tins, dated tallow tubes, lot codes on every batch, and the candle lid sticker replaced with a cream label and one gold line.
- Photos: Justin shoots each product family once, phone only, window-lit on cream linen, one hero and one detail shot per listing, and nothing on sequin fabric or the pegboard goes live.
- Packaging: crisp paper bands instead of crumpled kraft wraps, one plain gift bag, launch supplies bought in two tranches with $600 held back in the business account, and beeswax in 5 lb lots.
- The conversation with Iris: Justin tells her the site is the shop window and the booth is the workshop, the window holds twelve things, he runs labels, photos, prices, and quarterly numbers, new ideas debut on the Workbench page and earn a slot with ten inquiries, and he closes with "I decide what's on the site, you decide what gets made."
