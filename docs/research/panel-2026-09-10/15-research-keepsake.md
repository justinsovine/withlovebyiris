## 2. Top three sewn products by demand-to-competition ratio

1. **Memory bears, priced as a specialist.** Grief demand is durable and trend-proof. Etsy is crowded at $50 to $90, but specialist shops hold $125 to $200 and $350+ for fur coats ([Laura Lynn](https://memorybearsbylauralynn.com/memory-bear-pricing/)). Her photo-patch applique earns the specialist tier: $150 to $225 for 18 in with patch (est.), deposit up front, published lead times, Christmas cutoff mid-October ([Hold a Memory](https://holdamemory.com/faq/)).
2. **Memory quilts from clothing, not T-shirts.** Same $200 to $600 band as T-shirt quilts but far fewer sellers can do applique ([InsightAgent](https://www.insightagent.app/guides/selling-quilts-on-etsy)). Same buyer as the bear, so sell it as the upsell: a bear per grandchild, one quilt for the spouse.
3. **Baby quilts with birth-detail applique.** Steady demand, heavy competition, and $75 to $250 caps 8 to 20 hours of labor ([InsightAgent](https://www.insightagent.app/guides/selling-quilts-on-etsy)). Keep it as the entry product that later yields memory-bear customers; price at $200+, two patterns only.

## 3. Add: memory pillows

Same clothing, buyer, and pitch as a bear, at one to two hours of labor. Etsy sellers charge est. $35 to $85 with embroidered patches as upsells ([Etsy](https://www.etsy.com/market/memory_pillow)). A $60 option when a $175 bear is too much, made from the leftover shirt. A pet-loss variant (dog's blanket, paw-print applique) enters a market growing 9.12% a year that reports say is still ceramic and metal, not textile ([GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/01/16/3220372/28124/en/pet-memorial-products-market-outlook-report-2026-2032-lucrative-growth-opportunities-presented-by-personalized-design-led-offerings-that-blend-physical-and-digital-remembrance.html)).

## 4. Faith-forward positioning

Religious gifts are $7.28B growing 6.85% a year, North America 35.9%, personal devotion 51% of spend ([PMarketResearch](https://pmarketresearch.com/auto/religious-gifts-market/)); the top five firms hold 3.9%, so small makers are the norm ([GMInsights](https://www.gminsights.com/industry-analysis/religious-and-spiritual-products-market)). Buyers skew 25 to 54, and 45% of gift-givers in that band buy a religious item yearly ([PMarketResearch](https://pmarketresearch.com/auto/religious-gifts-market/)); Christian women over 45 control a growing share of wealth ([Lilly School 2026](https://philanthropy.indianapolis.iu.edu/news-events/news/_news/2026/christian-women-generosity-2026.html)); Faith Driven Consumers are 17% of US adults spending $2T ([Faith Driven Consumer](http://www.faithdrivenglobal.com/new_research_sheds_light_on_who_faith_driven_consumers_are_and_what_they_want)). Verdict: scripture narrows the bath-and-body audience slightly but widens the keepsake audience, since bereavement and baby gifts already carry a faith framing for rural Ohio buyers. Keep it on candles and men's balms; offer, do not force, on bears and quilts.

## 5. Stop

- T-shirt quilts, unless asked; per-block pricing races to $15 a shirt.
- Paw balm as a product line; at $10 it fights $12 Amazon tubes. Keep as a vet-counter goodwill item.
- Character bears as catalog items; no demand signal surfaced. Commission only.
