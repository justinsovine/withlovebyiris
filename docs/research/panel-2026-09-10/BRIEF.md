# Panel brief: Wholesome Living / With Love by Iris

Date: 2026-09-10. Location: Bethel, Ohio (Clermont County, ~30 mi east of Cincinnati). Owner: Iris McLaren, a solo maker, non-technical, sells at craft fair booths and by word of mouth. Her adult son (a software engineer) is building her an e-commerce site at withlovebyiris.com on Medusa v2 (Node headless commerce, Next.js storefront), self-hosted. Site is not live yet; product catalog is empty. He wants the panel to help streamline her business model and design the site's UX and user flows so it makes sales and retains customers. He suspects Etsy is too cutthroat and that local markets plus a direct-to-site following via marketing may be better, but wants honest analysis.

## Branding facts
- Her printed product labels already read "Wholesome Living, by ir!s" (stylized "ir!s"). Domain is withlovebyiris.com. Naming not yet reconciled; leading option is site = "With Love by Iris", "Wholesome Living" = the bath & body line name.
- Labels are watercolor botanical illustrations on cream/kraft, script + serif type. Palette cues: cream, kraft tan, sage green, honey gold, terracotta. Warm cottage botanical, not rustic farmhouse, not minimal.
- Scripture is a deliberate, visible thread: beeswax candle lids carry verses (Psalm 23, Isaiah 60:1-3, etc.), men's balms carry verses (1 Cor 16:13, Prov 20:7). Faith-forward brand.
- Her copy voice is warm, punny, personal ("Bee leave me when I say...", "Don't let them have a RUFF day"). She writes long Facebook posts about process and ingredients.

## Products observed (photos + her posts)
- Sulfate-free shampoo bars, 10 scents: Orange Cream, Rose of Sharon, Lavender Fields, Sandalwood, Honeysuckle Dew, Tutti Frutti, Vanilla Bean, Sunshine Bliss, Key Lime Pie, Cedarwood. Ingredients: kaolin clay, argan oil, shampoo base, essential oils, vitamin E, flower petal dust. Round bars wrapped in kraft paper with a watercolor label.
- Face & Beard Balm for men in jars: "Strong & Steadfast" (sandalwood, 1 Cor 16:13), "The Noble Man" (frankincense, Prov 20:7).
- Botanical clay mask, facial scrub, cooling mint muscle balm.
- "Peppermint Smack" lip gloss roll-ons, "natural alternative".
- "Paw-Fection" dog paw balm in a tube, $10, lick-safe, 7 ingredients (turmeric, black pepper, shea, vitamin E, coconut oil, beeswax, beef tallow). She is dropping samples at her vet hoping the vet will stock it. She explicitly wants to keep it cheap and will take a loss.
- 100% beeswax candles in black tins, no fragrance, scripture on the lid, marketed as "light it while you pray and read." Made for a May 2026 craft fair.
- Sewn goods: memory bears made from a deceased loved one's clothing with photo patches (custom, emotional, high value), character stuffed bears (a USPS postal bear in corduroy), quilts including baby quilts with birth details (name, date, weight, time) in applique. She is an experienced seamstress with decades of quilting.
- Bee/honeycomb themed gift bags used for packaging or gift sets.
- No bar soap observed (son originally assumed soap; unconfirmed).

## Constraints
- Solo maker, limited hours, does everything by hand. Photos are phone shots at her workbench.
- Small budget. Son provides the tech for free. Hosting is a $12 to 24/mo Linode.
- Ohio sales tax is origin-based for in-state sales. She has no LLC or insurance that we know of (unconfirmed).
- Local pickup and craft fairs are her current channels. Shipping is USPS from a rural post office.
- Site will have: catalog, cart, checkout (Stripe planned), customer accounts, order history, promo codes, local pickup and flat-rate shipping, email notifications. Custom flows (inquiry forms, subscriptions, bundles) can be built if the panel says they matter.

## What the panel must deliver
Each panelist writes a memo from their own expertise. Be concrete, opinionated, and specific to this maker. Cite real 2026 numbers where you can (fees, regulations, benchmarks) using web search; say when you are estimating. Disagree with the son's assumptions when warranted. No filler, no generic small-business platitudes. Write for the son, who will implement, and for Iris, who will run it.
