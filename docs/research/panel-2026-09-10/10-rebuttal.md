## Revised calls

- Catalog launches with 12 sellable SKUs: 5 shampoo scents plus 1 seasonal, 2 men's balms, 3 candle verses, 1 Paw-Fection two-pack.
- Lip gloss, face line, muscle balm, samplers, gift sets, and the Find Me At page are cut from v1.
- Etsy and all consignment are dropped; the vet is wholesale only, $6, 12-unit minimum, prepaid.
- Memory bears: one per month at $195, 50% deposit by draft order; five paid deposits is the year-two go/no-go.
- Postal and character bears move to inquiry-only under Custom Sewing.
- Fairs: six a year, each clearing $500 gross and $30/hr after COGS.
- Plan rebuilt around 10 hours a week; son logs actual hours for four weeks first.
- Site v1: catalog, guest checkout with pickup and flat rate, bear inquiry with deposit flow, three emails. Bundles, subscriptions, Facebook Shop, `/booth` deferred.
- Packaging: watercolor system on everything, plain "Iris" wordmark, candle lid redesigned, paper bands, one plain gift bag.
- Photos: cream or linen, window light, one hero plus one detail.
- Supplies in two tranches, $600 held back; beeswax in 5 lb lots.
- Legal spend ($650 to $750) precedes the first shipped order and any new product.

## Rules the son enforces

1. A product gets a page only if it wears the watercolor botanical label and a complete legal label.
2. A listing goes live only with a cream or linen, window-lit hero and one detail shot; no workbench photos.
3. The catalog holds 12 sellable SKUs including variants; a new one needs a retired one and 90 days of sales data.
4. Retail is at least 4x materials and wholesale 2x; the catalog rejects anything under.
5. Custom sewing pages show only bears he curated and sell only through the inquiry form with a 50% deposit.
6. One bear per month; the form closes when the month is booked.
7. Supplies come from the business account only, and only if $600 remains after.
8. Each quarter he pulls the numbers: under 10 units in 90 days, or a fair under $30/hr after COGS, is retired without appeal.
